good questions

Before you ask

How far will you travel?+

Anywhere inside about an hour of Tulsa at no extra charge — Broken Arrow, Jenks, Bixby, Owasso, Sand Springs, Claremore. Further than that, ask me and I'll quote the drive honestly.

How many photos will we get?+

Every frame worth keeping, edited. A thirty-minute session usually lands somewhere around thirty to fifty images; a full wedding day runs into the hundreds. I don't hold any back.

When is the best time of day?+

The last two hours before sunset, almost always. With small children, early morning is often the kinder choice — we'll pick the one that suits your family, not my portfolio.

What if it rains?+

Oklahoma weather gets one free reschedule, no questions and no fee. Overcast light is lovely, though — we only move for real storms.

Can we use the images for our business?+

Yes — brand and social sessions come with a commercial release covering your website, your ads and your channels. Just tell me at booking what you're planning.
