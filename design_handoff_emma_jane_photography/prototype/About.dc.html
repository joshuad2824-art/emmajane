I'm Emma Jane

I've lived in and around Tulsa most of my life, and I still think the best thing about it is the light at the end of a summer day — the way it comes sideways across a field off Route 66 and makes everybody look like they're remembering something.

Lifestyle photography is just a way of saying I'd rather photograph what's happening than arrange it. I'll give you something to do instead of somewhere to look. Walk that way, tell him what you were laughing about earlier, pick her up if she asks. The pictures come out of that.

Families, seniors, weddings, and the small shops that keep this town interesting — those are my people.
